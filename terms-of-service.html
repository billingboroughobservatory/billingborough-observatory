TimelapseUpload — Terms of Service

Last updated: 25 September 2026

These Terms of Service govern the use of the TimelapseUpload application operated by Billingborough Observatory.

TimelapseUpload is an automated application used to upload daily timelapse videos produced by the Billingborough Observatory AllSky camera to the Observatory's YouTube channel.

TimelapseUpload is provided for the operation and maintenance of Billingborough Observatory's automated astronomical timelapse service.

The application is authorised to access the associated YouTube account only for purposes connected with the operation of the automated timelapse upload service.
