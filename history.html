A village with an astronomical past

Edward Iszatt Essam was an astronomer associated with Billingborough in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. He lived and worked in the village, operating a drug store and Post Office.

Essam also established an astronomical observatory. Historical records indicate that it was well equipped, including substantial reflecting telescopes, and that Essam made observations of objects including Jupiter and Saturn.

The historic observatory ceased operation around 1907. Its precise location and surviving physical evidence remain subjects of interest for local and astronomical history.

A new chapter

Billingborough Observatory continues the village's astronomical story with modern telescopes, cameras and automated meteor detection.
