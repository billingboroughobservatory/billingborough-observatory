ASTRONOMICAL HERITAGE

Astronomy has returned to Billingborough

In the late nineteenth century, Edward Iszatt Essam was an astronomer associated with Billingborough. He operated the village Post Office and drug store and was also involved in astronomical observation and telescope making.

Historical records indicate that Essam's observatory was active in Billingborough around the turn of the twentieth century and appears to have ceased operation around 1907. Its precise location is still being investigated.

More than a century later, astronomical observation has returned to the village.
