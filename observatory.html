THE OBSERVATORY

Billingborough Observatory

A modern amateur astronomical observatory in Lincolnshire, combining traditional visual astronomy with astrophotography, automated cameras and citizen science.

A place for observing, imaging and discovery

The observatory brings together telescopes, astronomical cameras and automated systems to explore the night sky, record what happens above Billingborough and contribute observations to wider astronomical projects.

Exploring planets, the Moon and deep-sky objects throughout the night sky.

Daytime observing

Astrophotography

OBSERVING

Exploring the night sky

The observatory is used for visual astronomical observing, from the Moon and planets to deep-sky objects and the changing appearance of the night sky throughout the year.

Observing combines the simple experience of looking through a telescope with the opportunity to study astronomical objects in greater detail.

ASTROPHOTOGRAPHY

Imaging the universe

Modern astronomical cameras allow the observatory to record objects that would otherwise be difficult to see by eye. Astrophotography is an important part of the observatory's work and provides a growing visual record of the night sky from Lincolnshire.

The observatory combines telescopes, astronomical cameras and image-processing techniques to explore everything from nearby planets to distant deep-sky objects.

AUTOMATED ASTRONOMY

Watching the sky when nobody is looking

Not all astronomical observation requires someone to be sitting at the telescope. Cameras and computer-controlled systems allow the observatory to monitor the sky automatically.
