THE CAMERA

Recording the changing sky

The Allsky Camera operates automatically throughout 24-hours, capturing a sequence of images of the entire visible sky.

The images are processed on a Raspberry Pi and assembled into a daily timelapse. The completed timelapse is then published automatically to YouTube.

This creates a continuous visual record of the sky above the observatory and allows individual days and nights to be viewed long after they have passed.
