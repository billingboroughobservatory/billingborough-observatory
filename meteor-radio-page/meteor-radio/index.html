THE STATION

How the system works

Billingborough Observatory uses a Raspberry Pi and RTL-SDR receiver to monitor the 143.050 MHz GRAVES transmitter. When a meteoroid creates an ionised trail high in the atmosphere, that trail can briefly reflect the transmitter's signal towards the receiving station.

MeteorRadio by Richard Bassom records these short-lived echoes and analyses their signal strength, duration and frequency behaviour. The resulting observations are processed on the observatory's Ubuntu system before the public data shown here is generated.
