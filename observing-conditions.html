HOW TO READ IT

What matters for astronomy?

Cloud cover

The first thing to check. Lower cloud cover means more of the sky is available for observing and imaging.

Seeing

Seeing describes atmospheric turbulence. Lower values in arcseconds generally mean steadier air and sharper planetary and lunar views.

Humidity

High humidity can increase the risk of dew forming on telescope optics, cameras and other exposed equipment.
